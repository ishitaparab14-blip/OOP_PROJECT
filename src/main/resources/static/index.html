Transparent Workflow

How CampusFind Protects & Reconnects

A verified three-step protocol that eliminates fake claims and streamlines retrieval.

1

1. Report or Catalog

Students publish lost notifications or register items they found across labs, libraries, hostels, and cafeterias.

2

2. Smart Match & Claim

Our algorithm recommends matching items. Real owners submit verifiable private details (serial numbers, wallpapers, markings).

3

3. Admin Verification

Campus administrators verify proof, approve valid claims, prevent duplicate handovers, and notify both parties.
